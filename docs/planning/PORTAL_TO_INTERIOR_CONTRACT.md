# Portal-to-Interior Contract

Status: permanent planning constraint; Phase 1 static semantic foundation only
Applies to: the Phase 1 semantic DOM, the planned Phase 2 interior system, and any separately authorized Phase 3/4 CRT opening and integration

> THE PORTAL IS NOT THE FINALE. IT IS THE THRESHOLD.

> THE ENTIRE WEBSITE IS THE SPECTACLE — EXPRESSED THROUGH DIFFERENT INTENSITIES, MATERIALS AND FORMS.

The CRT opening must resolve into a continuing Quantum operating world, not a spectacle followed by an ordinary website. This contract defines that boundary without authorizing Phase 2, cinematic media, animation, scroll choreography, WebGL, sticky scenes, or any other opening runtime during Phase 1.

## Authorization boundary

- Phase 1 may provide stable semantic chapter boundaries, intrinsic document flow, accessible content, static scene-ready wrappers, and this planning contract.
- Phase 2 may begin only from a human-accepted Phase 1 SHA and under separate authorization. Its interior system must choreograph around the semantic DOM rather than replace it.
- Phase 3/4 CRT production and integration remain separately gated. This document constrains their eventual handoff; it does not authorize them.
- The existing CRT portal geometry and physical-versus-DOM ownership rules remain governed by [CRT_PORTAL_AND_TYPOGRAPHY_CONTRACT.md](./CRT_PORTAL_AND_TYPOGRAPHY_CONTRACT.md).

## Physical to semantic handoff

The eventual continuous sequence is:

```text
physical CRT glass fills the viewport
-> cabinet leaves the frame
-> physical raster flattens
-> scanlines reduce
-> phosphor softness reduces
-> overscan recedes
-> rendered interface becomes native semantic DOM
-> WHERE DO YOU ENTER? becomes crisp HTML
-> navigation appears
```

The physical raster may distort only until semantic takeover. Identical physical and DOM text must never be visible simultaneously. Once live semantic DOM owns the interface, it must remain crisp and must never receive barrel distortion, raster deformation, scanline distortion, or phosphor softness.

## Continuity and scroll rules

- There is no blank bridge, generic grey fade, abrupt 4:3-to-widescreen snap, or permanent letterboxing.
- Entry requires no extra scroll gesture. There is one native browser document scroll root and no nested full-page scroll container.
- Native browser scroll position is always the source of truth. Future visual progress may derive from it but may not cancel, replace, queue, or detach from it.
- Reverse scrolling reconstructs the same states cleanly in reverse, without a second timeline, delayed queue, discontinuity, or stale physical/DOM layer.
- Semantic content, landmarks, navigation, focus order, links, and history remain browser-native and usable without the cinematic layer.

## Interior continuity

The physical proving field transforms into the digital Quantum interior. The interior must retain visual continuity, physical-to-digital consequence, authored chapter transitions, and spectacle delivered in paced waves of differing intensity, material, and form.

The handoff must not resolve into `spectacular CRT -> ordinary website`. Homepage chapters remain independent semantic document regions that can later support full-width scenes, media planes, spatial foreground/background relationships, negative space, and distinct mobile compositions. Their accessible reading order and intrinsic height remain valid when no choreography runs.

## Reduced motion and runtime failure

Reduced motion bypasses the cinematic runtime and presents a static dormant proving field, powered-off CRT, dormant cable, semantic hero/interface, and normal document flow. The same semantic experience is immediately available when JavaScript or cinematic media is unavailable. No essential meaning or action depends on motion or a portal transition.

## Supporting routes

Supporting routes never replay the full CRT opening. They inherit its materiality, typography, transition grammar, visual world, and authored composition at an appropriate lower intensity. They remain native semantic documents and must not become generic card stacks merely because the opening is absent.

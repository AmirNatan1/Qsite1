## Existing content by route

### For industry

The public source begins with the operational need, keeps ownership and evidence
criteria explicit, then follows Frame → Source → Assess → Test → Decide. It may
support a defined challenge or ongoing innovation context and names CHAMP only as
an approved industry programme context. No partner roster, facility, metric,
commercial outcome, or guaranteed result is present.

### For startups

The source requires MVP+ capability, a credible industrial use case, a deployable
team, evidence criteria, and organisational readiness. Its route is Readiness →
Relevance → Design → Field → Evidence. It explicitly states that participation
does not guarantee a pilot, procurement agreement, or investment. SPARK remains
closed with no alternate registration path or future date.

### Industries

The only public industries are:

1. Automotive & Mobility
2. Logistics & Supply Chain
3. Industry 4.0 / Advanced Manufacturing
4. Energy & Infrastructure

The current two-column domain list is publication-safe but visually card-like.
That is a Phase 5B design problem, not permission to alter the list in Phase 5A.
Defense and dual-use are absent.

### Proof

The public collection is enforced at exactly one record: **Maradin — Dynamic
Ground Projection**. The index contains no placeholders, anonymous records,
confidential suggestions, invented scale, metrics, or “coming soon” filler.

### Maradin

The approved record separates challenge, technology, test design, execution,
evidence, and next step. Numeric public claims are rejected in source. Existing
media is governed by byte-identical provenance records. Visible vehicle and
Hyundai/CRADLE context must be treated as documentary fact, not endorsement or
proof of procurement/commercial success.

### SPARK

Public status is exactly **Applications closed** and `applicationOpen` is false.
The route contains no application form, waitlist, date, cohort statistic, or
guaranteed POC language. The current continuation into programme context avoids a
false application affordance.

### About

The exact H1 authority is already present. The route explains Quantum's operating
position between industry and technology and names Herzliya as context. Public
team, partner, update, and metric collections remain empty. No qFund people or
unverified roster appears.

### Contact

The exact H1 and three required intent groups are present. `contactDestination`
is deliberately `null`; the route therefore cannot publish an email, telephone,
form, backend, response-time promise, or simulated submission. Verification of a
real destination is a genuine Phase 5B content dependency.

### 404

The route is a real semantic error document with Home recovery and no media. The
line “This signal goes nowhere.” requires a human tone decision because the new
brief prohibits a joke that weakens the brand. Phase 5A does not silently rewrite
it.

## Current visual-system audit

The routes are functionally safe but not an acceptable Phase 5B endpoint:

- Eight routes share the same `PageHero` + editorial list/grid + closing CTA
  grammar.
- Industries is effectively a four-card grid.
- For industry and For startups lean heavily on capability grids and can read as
  service pages.
- Maradin contains documentary media inside rounded interface panels rather than
  allowing the evidence to establish the page.
- The same radial magenta glow, eyebrow dot, grey surface, and thin rule recur too
  uniformly.

Phase 5A preserves this baseline on public URLs. Local-only prototypes will test
distinct route identities without becoming public output.

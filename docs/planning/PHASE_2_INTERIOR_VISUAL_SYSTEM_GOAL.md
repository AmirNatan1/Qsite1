# Phase 2 Interior Visual System Goal

Status: Phase 2A creative-preproduction candidate; pending human review

Authority: additive to the accepted Phase 1 baseline; does not authorize Phase 2B

Production boundary: no production source or public asset changes

## Creative objective

Define and visually prove the post-portal Quantum experience as one continuous operating world. The physical CRT is the threshold, not the finale. Its raster, conductive cable and 4:3 picture boundary must transform into a contemporary semantic field whose spectacle continues through composition, consequence, material, pacing and documentary evidence.

The intended experience is:

```text
CONDUCTION -> APERTURE -> FIELD -> EVIDENCE -> DECISION
```

The target character is **precision instrument × industrial documentary × editorial architecture**. It must feel authored and spatial without resembling a terminal, dashboard, HUD, game interface, Web3 visualization or SaaS landing page.

## Phase 2A scope

Phase 2A produces a human-reviewable creative system before runtime implementation:

- a single post-portal creative thesis;
- a chapter-by-chapter homepage storyboard;
- a causal transition and motion grammar;
- desktop, mobile and reduced-motion keyframes;
- a deliberately small sticky/spatial policy;
- a real-media requirements brief;
- a concise supporting-route strategy;
- an isolated static design lab;
- compact contact-sheet evidence and a machine-readable evidence manifest;
- production-isolation, build and repository-impact verification.

The accepted homepage chapter order remains:

1. ENTRY
2. BUILT WITH INDUSTRY
3. METHOD
4. INDUSTRIES
5. PROOF
6. PROGRAMMES
7. CONVERSION

CURRENT SIGNAL is absent because no publication-approved current item exists.

## Non-goals

Phase 2A does not:

- alter the production homepage or supporting routes;
- implement the CRT film, portal handoff or production motion runtime;
- introduce scroll scrubbing, sticky sections, synthetic scrolling or horizontal scroll containers;
- add React, Three.js, WebGL, GSAP, canvas frameworks or a custom scroll engine;
- change approved copy, route architecture, metadata or publication governance;
- fabricate partners, metrics, proof, facilities, workers, programmes, contact destinations or industrial media;
- publish or deploy the design lab;
- authorize Phase 2B.

## Binding constraints

### Continuity

- The physical raster yields to crisp semantic HTML without doubled text or a blank bridge.
- A persistent visual cause travels from one chapter into the next.
- The 4:3 boundary is an origin, not a repeated badge or universal container.
- The design alternates peaks, editorial restraint and breathing space.

### Scroll

- Native document scrolling remains the only source of truth.
- No wheel cancellation, scroll snapping, nested full-page scroller, synthetic inertia or gesture gate is permitted.
- Fast and reverse scrolling must resolve directly from current document progress.
- Any eventual sticky treatment must enter and exit visibly and proportionally.

### Content and media

- Exactly four industries, two programmes and one public proof remain visible.
- Maradin is the only approved documentary proof.
- Empty public partner, team, metric and update collections remain absent.
- Missing media is expressed as an authored material aperture plus a documented shot requirement, never a fake image or visible placeholder.
- Magenta is reserved for live conduction, active routing and consequential state.

### Accessibility and typography

- The accepted semantic DOM, heading order, links, keyboard operation and focus behavior remain authoritative.
- Meaning never depends on decorative media, motion, hover or pointer position.
- Reduced motion receives separately composed static scenes in normal flow.
- Syne, Newsreader and Inter retain their accepted roles.
- Copy remains content-height-driven and safe at 200% text zoom.

### Technical and performance envelope

- Eventual implementation should rely on semantic DOM, CSS, SVG where structurally justified, approved image/video and small deterministic transforms.
- Transform, opacity and masks are preferred over scroll-linked layout work.
- Decorative motion stops when a chapter is not active.
- Mobile must not carry unnecessary video decode or excessive compositing layers.
- The design lab remains outside `src/` and `public/` and must never appear in `dist/`.

## Success definition

The Phase 2A package succeeds only if a reviewer can answer yes to all of the following:

- The experience remains extraordinary after the CRT.
- The portal reads as a threshold rather than an intro ending.
- Every chapter has a distinct visual idea while belonging to one world.
- METHOD feels like consequential transformation rather than five information cards.
- The industries read as environments, not a taxonomy grid.
- Maradin creates a confident shift from abstraction to documentary reality.
- PROGRAMMES creates intentional calm rather than a marketing-card pause.
- CONVERSION feels like the field resolving into a clear next step.
- Mobile is independently authored and reduced motion remains premium.
- Nothing depends on fabricated content, excessive motion or a heavy runtime.

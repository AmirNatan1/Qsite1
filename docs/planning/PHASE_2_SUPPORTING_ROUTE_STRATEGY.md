# Phase 2 Supporting-Route Strategy

Status: concise Phase 2A direction; no production redesign authorization

Supporting routes do not replay the CRT. Each begins inside the Operating Field with one authored overture, one restrained signature behavior and the same conductive datum, material family, typography and five-verb transition grammar. Semantic structure and fast load remain primary.

| Route | Overture | Signature behavior | Restraint / publication boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/for-partners/` | A broad operating constraint closes into a precise challenge boundary | The datum becomes an ownership-and-criteria edge, then releases toward METHOD | No partner wall, logo strip, metrics or invented relationship language |
| `/for-startups/` | A technology route enters from outside the field and meets a real-context boundary | The route narrows from SOURCE toward ASSESS and TEST | No guaranteed POC, procurement, investment, application form or inflated readiness claim |
| `/industries/` | Four material horizons are visible as one connected territory | Each domain receives one authored environmental passage in native vertical flow | Exactly four industries; technology categories remain cross-industry and subordinate |
| `/pocs/` | One evidence aperture opens in a large quiet field | The sole Maradin record expands from index to documentary threshold | No empty case slots, filters, sample records or fake scale |
| `/pocs/maradin/` | Approved documentary media establishes the real field immediately | Editorial aperture edges guide the record from challenge through test and evidence | Use only approved Maradin content/media; no KPI, deployment or commercial-success implication |
| `/spark/` | A single startup route reaches a visibly closed programme boundary | The route continues into programme context without exposing an application surface | `Applications closed`; no waitlist, dates, cohort claims or guaranteed outcome |
| `/about/` | The institutional horizon becomes a tall editorial spine | Material depth and measured type establish purpose and operating model | No team placeholders, metrics, partner claims or unsupported history |
| `/contact/` | The conductive datum reaches its quiet endpoint plane | Approved internal audience routes align into a stable next-step structure | No form, email or external destination until a verified public destination exists |

## Shared rules

- One meaningful H1, logical heading order and normal document flow per route.
- One authored overture and at most one signature behavior; no route becomes a smaller homepage spectacle stack.
- No full CRT replay, route-specific loading film or input hijacking.
- Reduced motion uses the resolved static overture and approved poster media.
- Mobile receives a vertical composition rather than a desktop crop.
- The datum may change function but may not become a progress meter or decorative border.
- Route transitions use Conduct, Focus, Cross, Resolve and Release only where they communicate real structure.

## System-coherence proof

The routes share these invariants:

- Dark V2 proving black, graphite, restrained warm shadow, editorial rules and documentary light;
- Syne for decisive states, Newsreader for contextual/documentary meaning and Inter for navigation/metadata;
- Conduct, Focus, Cross, Resolve and Release as the only motion grammar;
- one H1, native document flow and immediate semantic availability;
- settled site chrome, current-route indication, skip/focus safety and accessible mobile navigation;
- no sticky route chapters, scroll capture, synthetic scrolling, page-transition runtime or continuous animation;
- reduced-motion and no-JS states designed as complete static compositions;
- magenta reserved for live consequence, status, target or focus;
- no public claim, identity, asset or destination without explicit publication authority.

They remain distinct because each route changes at least four of the following: dominant geometry, spatial pressure, chapter rhythm, datum function, motion mode, media role, conversion behavior and visual density.

## Performance-cost interpretation

- **Minimal** — CSS-only route treatment, no route JavaScript or media.
- **Low** — at most one observer or a bounded progress mapper, no media decode.
- **Medium** — a small bounded mapper with multiple spatial layers, or user-initiated documentary video decode.
- **High** — prohibited for supporting routes.

All estimates are raw incremental source budgets. No route may add a third-party dependency, continuous frame loop, frame sequence, WebGL, canvas runtime or custom scroll library.

## Publication-risk interpretation

- **Low** — content is already approved and contains no identity, metric or destination gap.
- **Moderate** — spatial framing could imply scale, access, relationship or outcome if handled carelessly.
- **High** — a factual blocker exists and must remain visibly unresolved, as with Contact's missing destination.

Risk classification does not authorize mitigation through invented copy. The correct response to an unverified fact is omission or an honest static boundary.

## Television design brief

The selected object must read immediately as a substantial late-1980s/1990s CRT television, approximately 28–32 inches, 0.78–0.95 metres wide, 0.62–0.78 metres high and 0.65–0.85 metres deep. It sits directly on the proving-ground surface and feels heavy, deep and grounded.

Required design properties:

- unmistakable 4:3 convex CRT screen;
- near-black charcoal injection-moulded ABS cabinet;
- era-appropriate rounded corners, thick bezel and deep tube bulge;
- believable cabinet thickness, seams, rear/side ventilation, feet and lower weight;
- restrained lower control/speaker band with physical controls and one tiny power indicator;
- credible rear service geometry, strain relief and grounded cable entry;
- high-quality curvature, bevel hierarchy, smooth shading, contact shadow and manufacturing logic;
- smoked off-state glass with thickness, subtle neutral reflections and faint internal phosphor distinction;
- subtle cared-for age variation without grime, damage or horror styling.

It must not become a flat panel, monitor on a stand, futuristic terminal, cyberpunk object, minimalist box or Aperture Station derivative. Visible faceting, paper-thin walls, flat screen glass, placeholder controls, noisy renders and impossible cable connections are rejection conditions.

## Power-on contract

The causal sequence is:

1. Current reaches the cabinet connection.
2. One small physical indicator responds.
3. A restrained, coherent CRT wake occurs using only a necessary subset of degaussing distortion, a brief central glow or line, low phosphor bloom, and raster warming.
4. The raster stabilizes with subtle scanlines and slightly lifted black level.
5. The Quantum signal interface appears.

The first readable physical-screen state is `QUANTUM HUB`, followed by the five-stage route `FRAME SOURCE ASSESS TEST DECIDE`, then the single approved status `TEST ROUTE AVAILABLE`. Multiple status messages, tiny fictional labels, extended static, colour bars, VHS errors, horror noise, global RGB splitting and sci-fi HUD treatment are prohibited.

The full `WHERE DO YOU ENTER?` composition belongs primarily to semantic DOM after entry.

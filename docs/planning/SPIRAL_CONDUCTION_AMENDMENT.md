# Spiral Conduction Amendment

Status: authoritative creative amendment
Accepted for Phase 0 feasibility: 17 August 2026

## Supersession

Spiral Conduction supersedes every earlier opening-scene instruction involving:

- a free-moving magenta point in the landscape;
- magenta beacons;
- floating magenta objects;
- a signal moving through open air;
- a pre-lit magenta cable, environment, Field Unit, control, seam, aperture, or screen.

The accepted Proving Field, original Field Unit, controlled camera approach, power-on, screen/interface activation, semantic DOM handoff, portal continuity, authored mobile 2.5D mode, and reduced-motion alternative remain in force.

If an earlier planning sentence conflicts with this file, this file wins.

## Creative statement

One physical cable carries an external industrial challenge through a mathematically clean spiral into Quantum's testing instrument. The environment and device begin dormant. Scroll introduces one cumulative warm-magenta conduction front at the cable's outer terminus. The front advances toward the centre while the camera changes angle; the cable behind it remains active and the cable ahead remains neutral. The current reaches the Field Unit only when the major camera movement is essentially complete. The unit then powers on once, exposes sparse Quantum operating information, and opens the original Q-interface portal into semantic content.

The effect must feel like energy or data moving through a real cable, not a line drawn on top of a scene.

## Opening composition

The composition contains exactly:

- a dark industrial proving ground;
- one original Quantum Field Unit at the precise compositional centre;
- one thin physical wire laid flat across and subtly following the terrain;
- one mathematically clean spiral leading toward the Field Unit;
- neutral grazing light sufficient to read the cable and unit;
- semantic hero content and audience actions in DOM.

At progress `0.00`:

- the environment is dormant;
- the Field Unit is completely powered off;
- the interface/screen is black or materially inactive;
- the cable sheath is graphite/charcoal;
- there is no emissive cable core;
- there is no magenta reflection, environmental glow, beacon, floating particle, illuminated endpoint, illuminated device element, pulse, or electric arc.

The mandatory visible keyboard-focus indicator is the only exemption from the zero-magenta initial state.

## Spiral geometry

The Phase 0 geometry study compares approximately 2.25, 2.5, and 2.75 rotations before selecting one reviewed geometry.

The preferred family is an Archimedean spiral:

```text
r(theta) = a + b * theta
```

where constant `b` produces even radial spacing between turns. The implementation may transform this planar curve to follow terrain height, but the plan-view spacing must remain deliberate and legible.

Required physical properties:

- one continuous cable only;
- centre precisely aligned with the Field Unit;
- approximately 2.25-2.75 rotations;
- consistent spacing between turns;
- outer terminus entering or approaching the visual perimeter;
- inner terminus visibly connected to a credible power/data port;
- no branch, loose coil, floating segment, disconnected end, or decorative second wire;
- restrained, believable material thickness;
- ground contact with subtle terrain conformance and contact shadow;
- sufficient arrival-view contrast through material and neutral grazing light;
- visual elegance without industrial bulk or ornamental dominance.

The spiral has three simultaneous roles:

1. physical object in the proving environment;
2. principal compositional line leading the eye toward the Field Unit;
3. metaphor for an external challenge being routed into Quantum.

The geometry study must mark:

- exact spiral centre;
- Field Unit footprint;
- outer terminus;
- inner connector and port;
- number of turns;
- turn spacing;
- cable thickness;
- proposed camera axis;
- hero-copy safe area;
- navigation safe area.

## Dormant cable material

Before activation:

- the sheath is graphite/charcoal;
- the core is non-emissive;
- there is no pulse or travelling marker;
- the outer endpoint is not illuminated;
- there is no electricity arc or science-fiction energy effect;
- visibility comes only from neutral grazing light, contact shadow, and small material contrast.

The cable must remain physically credible throughout activation: the magenta state is a current within or immediately on the cable, not an overlay detached from its volume.

## Conduction behavior

As normalized document progress advances past `0.08`, one conduction front enters the cable at its outer terminus and moves toward the centre.

The state is cumulative:

- cable behind the leading edge remains illuminated;
- cable ahead of the leading edge remains dormant;
- the boundary advances continuously;
- there is exactly one front;
- there is no repeated pulse train;
- there is no moving magenta ball;
- no particle, spark, arc, or light leaves the cable;
- the outer terminus does not remain a decorative beacon.

The active cable uses:

- a restrained warm core around `#f06ba0`;
- Quantum magenta influence around `#d82b72`;
- a low-opacity local halo;
- an extremely subtle reflection only on immediately adjacent ground;
- no broad neon wash, saturated tube, or Tron-like effect.

For a browser-native 2.5D renderer, the conduction state must be derived from a single normalized value representing cumulative cable length. It may be implemented through an original masked layer, path-length mapping, canvas sampling, or another deterministic browser-native technique. The result must preserve physical thickness, contact, and the dormant/active material boundary.

## Camera and conduction synchronization

The working normalized sequence is binding for Phase 0 evidence:

| Progress | Visual state |
|---:|---|
| `0.00-0.08` | Entire system dormant; no magenta illumination |
| `0.08-0.16` | Current begins entering the outermost wire |
| `0.16-0.68` | Current advances inward while the camera begins and completes its controlled changing-angle movement |
| `0.68-0.76` | Camera reaches near-frontal alignment as current reaches the final inner turn |
| `0.76-0.80` | Current enters the physical Field Unit connector |
| `0.80-0.87` | Field Unit powers on in a restrained sequence |
| `0.84-0.91` | Screen/interface becomes readable |
| `0.89-0.97` | Camera enters the activated screen/Q-interface surface |
| `0.97-1.00` | Semantic operating surface fully owns the viewport |

The key dramatic rule is absolute: the current reaches the Field Unit only when the major camera movement is essentially complete. The Field Unit must not power on early.

The camera and visual state are computed from absolute document progress. They do not depend on elapsed time, wheel-event count, direction-specific animation queues, or an additional gesture.

## Field Unit power-on

When the conduction front enters the physical connector, the device powers on once in this order:

1. a small connector response appears;
2. power travels internally toward the screen/Q aperture;
3. minimal controlled edge light reaches the device surface;
4. the screen/interface wakes;
5. sparse Quantum operating information becomes readable;
6. the portal becomes available.

The Field Unit should feel powered, not decorated. Do not illuminate every seam, border, port, or control. Avoid gaming-terminal, crypto-device, consumer-laptop, and science-fiction-prop cues.

Suggested screen language:

```text
challenge detected
context received
test route available
```

and:

```text
Frame
Source
Assess
Test
Decide
```

Essential site meaning remains semantic DOM content or has a semantic DOM equivalent. Review artwork may contain interface labels as part of the concept, but production must not make required public information available only inside an image, canvas, texture, or video.

## Portal

The Field Unit's screen and Q-derived aperture form one original Quantum mechanism. After activation, the camera enters that interface and the native semantic operating surface assumes ownership.

The portal must not resemble:

- entering a laptop;
- a rectangular amber wipe;
- a desktop operating system;
- a science-fiction wormhole;
- a generic glowing circle.

The portal-alignment study must demonstrate:

- final visual interface bounds;
- activated Q/screen surface bounds;
- crop and foreground edge;
- interface safe region;
- first DOM heading position;
- navigation reveal zone;
- a credible frame in which visual and DOM ownership overlap.

The production handoff has:

- no blank bridge;
- no additional wheel gesture;
- no nested full-page scroller;
- no URL mutation at the threshold;
- no timer-dependent reveal;
- no iframe;
- no core site content trapped in canvas or a screen texture.

## Reverse behavior

Reverse scrolling reconstructs the exact inverse sequence:

1. the native operating surface recedes into the matched portal state;
2. the Field Unit screen powers down;
3. internal and controlled edge illumination turns off;
4. conduction retracts from the inner connector outward along the spiral;
5. the cable returns to its dormant graphite state;
6. the camera returns to the arrival composition.

Every displayed state is a direct function of normalized document progress. No queued reverse animation, settling delay, repeated pulse, network-dependent blank, or time-owned power-down is allowed.

## Desktop, tablet, and short-height behavior

Desktop keeps the complete spiral relationship and controlled changing-angle movement. The working sequence uses approximately 440svh total section height and approximately 340svh of effective travel, subject to later production validation.

Tablet reduces environment layers and camera range while retaining:

- a readable full spiral;
- cumulative outside-in conduction;
- connector arrival after major movement;
- restrained power-on;
- the same portal logic.

Short-height desktop framing must keep the navigation, semantic hero, cable, and Field Unit from colliding at 650-700px viewport height. It may shorten terrain and reduce copy scale but cannot pre-light the scene or hide the cable's physical relationship.

## Mobile

Mobile keeps the same story:

```text
dormant cable
-> cumulative inward conduction
-> device power-on
-> interface activation
-> portal
```

It uses an independently authored premium 2.5D composition with:

- no broad three-dimensional orbit;
- fewer owned layers;
- a larger Field Unit;
- a clearly legible spiral;
- shorter movement and scroll travel;
- no desktop crop;
- no long frame preload;
- no hover dependency;
- natural vertical touch scroll;
- the same exact reverse-state logic.

Phase 0 evidence targets both 360x800 and 390x844.

## Reduced motion

Reduced motion loads no cinematic video or frame sequence and does not create the interactive conduction renderer.

The designed static poster shows:

- the dormant proving ground;
- one physical unlit spiral cable;
- the powered-off Field Unit;
- neutral material lighting;
- complete semantic hero copy and actions in DOM.

It contains no simulated conduction, camera movement, portal rush, pulsation, or autoplay. The user can enter the semantic site immediately. Focus states remain visible and accessible.

## Creative rejection conditions

The creative gate cannot ACCEPT an evidence set that contains any of the following:

- initial magenta environmental light;
- pre-lit Field Unit or screen;
- a moving magenta point, orb, beacon, particle, pulse train, spark, arc, or airborne line;
- a disconnected, floating, branching, doubled, or loosely coiled cable;
- conduction that lights the entire cable at once;
- a device that powers on before the connector event;
- a broad neon wash or magenta decoration on every seam;
- a laptop, desktop OS, wormhole, or generic glowing-circle portal;
- a blank/timed bridge, nested full-page scroll, URL mutation, or extra wheel gesture;
- a mobile desktop crop or long eager frame sequence;
- a reduced-motion experience that loads or simulates the cinematic sequence;
- reference, scraped, stock-product, or another company's creative material.
